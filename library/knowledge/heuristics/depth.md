Suggestions to consider. Flat walls are the most common thing that makes a block build look unfinished.

## Layers
- **Think in at least three layers:** a frame (corners, posts, pillars) that sticks out, the wall infill, and openings set back. Why: each step creates a shadow line, and shadow is what shows form in renders and in game.
- **One block of depth is usually enough** at house scale. Large builds need 2–3 blocks to read from a distance.

## Where to add depth
- **Corners and posts:** logs, pillars or a contrasting block pushed out by one.
- **Base course:** a plinth one block proud of the wall (or a different material) grounds the building.
- **Floor lines:** a band of trim or overhanging upper storey (jetty) between storeys.
- **Cornice/eaves:** upside-down stairs under the roof edge.
- **Buttresses** on long or tall walls, at bay intervals (see heuristics/windows).

## Partial blocks
- Stairs, slabs, walls, trapdoors and fences give **sub-block depth**. Use them for sills, lintels, shutters and trim instead of only full blocks.
- **Vary texture slightly** (a 70/30 mix of two close blocks) on big surfaces; keep trim and frames as a single clean block so they still read as lines.

## Restraint
- Too many layers everywhere become noise. Keep some calm wall area so the details have something to stand out against.
